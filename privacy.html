AA Pin the Needle — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Who we are

AA Pin the Needle is a mobile game published on Google Play by Canvider ("we"). We are the data controller for the game under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Türkiye's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK).

  • Developer: Canvider
  • E-mail: info@canvider.com

This policy covers the Android version of the game.

In short

  • You never create an account, and we never ask for your name, e-mail or contacts.
  • Your progress and settings stay on your phone. We never receive them.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google processes device data, such as your advertising ID, to show and measure ads.
  • Ads are personalized only if you agree, and you can change your answer at any time.
  • If you buy Remove ads, Google Play handles the payment. We never see your card details.

What the game keeps on your phone

The game saves a small file and a few settings on your phone so you can pick up where you left off:

  • the levels you cleared or skipped, your day streak, and whether you cleared today's daily level;
  • your sound and vibration settings;
  • your answer to the personalized-ads question, how many levels you cleared since the last full-screen ad, and whether you bought Remove ads.

None of this is sent to us. We run no server, and the game contains no analytics or crash-reporting tools.

If Android backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include this file in your Google account's backup. Google's terms for that backup apply.

Advertising with Google AdMob

The game shows ads through Google AdMob, a service provided by Google: banners on the menu screens, a full-screen ad between some levels, and an optional video you can watch to skip a level.

When the game requests an ad, Google's software in the game collects and processes:

  • your device's advertising ID;
  • your IP address, and the approximate location Google derives from it;
  • device and app information, such as phone model, Android version, language and app version;
  • how you interact with ads, such as views and taps.

Google uses this data to show ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure how ads perform, and detect fraud and abuse. With your consent, Google also uses it to show ads based on your interests. Without consent you still see ads, but they are not personalized.

We do not receive this data ourselves. AdMob reports only totals to us, such as how many ads were shown.

Google explains its use of this data in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices about ads

  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the game shows Google's consent form the first time you open it. No ads are requested until you answer.
  • Everywhere else, including Türkiye, the game asks whether ads may be personalized. Both answers look the same, and the game plays the same either way. No ads are requested until you answer; if you do not allow it, ads are not personalized.
  • Change your answer at any time under Ad choices on the game's title screen.
  • On your phone, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off ad personalization, in Android's settings (usually Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Remove ads turns off banners and the full-screen ads between levels, and skipping a level no longer asks you to watch a video.

The Remove ads purchase

You can buy Remove ads once, through Google Play. Google Play processes the payment and your payment details under the Google Play Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

The game receives only Google Play's confirmation that your Google account owns the purchase. It checks this each time it starts, so the purchase carries over to a new phone. In Google Play Console we can see order records, such as the order number, date, price and country, which we use only for accounting, tax and refunds.

International transfers

Google processes ad and purchase data on servers in many countries, including the United States.

  • From the EEA and Switzerland, Google relies on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses and the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.
  • From Türkiye, transfers abroad fall under KVKK Article 9 and rely on the safeguards Google provides in its terms.

How long data is kept

  • On your phone: until you delete the game or clear its data in Android's settings.
  • At Google: for the periods in How Google retains data it collects.
  • Order records in Play Console: as long as tax and accounting law requires.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may ask to access, correct, delete or restrict your data, object to its processing, receive a copy, and withdraw consent at any time.

Under KVKK Article 11 you may ask whether your data is processed and for information about it, learn its purpose and the recipients in Türkiye and abroad, ask for correction or deletion, object to a result reached by automated analysis alone, and claim compensation for damage from unlawful processing.

We keep no personal data about you on our own systems, so most requests concern Google. You can manage them directly in My Ad Center and Google's privacy tools. For anything else, e-mail info@canvider.com; we answer within 30 days.

You can also complain to a data protection authority: in Türkiye the Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK), in the EEA the authority in your country.

Children

The game is meant for players aged 13 and over and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly process children's personal data. If you are a parent and believe your child has used the game, contact us and we will help.
