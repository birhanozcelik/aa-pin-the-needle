AA Pin the Needle — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Who we are

AA Pin the Needle is a mobile game published on Google Play and Apple's App Store by Canvider ("we"). We are the data controller for the game under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Türkiye's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK).

  • Developer: Canvider
  • E-mail: info@canvider.com

This policy covers both versions of the game: Android and iOS (iPhone). Where they differ, it says so.

In short

  • You never create an account, and we never ask for your name, e-mail or contacts.
  • Your progress and settings stay on your phone. We never receive them.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google processes device data, such as your advertising identifier, to show and measure ads.
  • Ads are personalized only if you agree, and you can change your answer at any time. On iPhone, the game uses Apple's advertising identifier only if you allow tracking when iOS asks.
  • On Android, if you buy Remove ads, Google Play handles the payment. We never see your card details. The iOS version has no purchases.

What the game keeps on your phone

The game saves a small file and a few settings on your phone so you can pick up where you left off:

  • the levels you cleared or skipped, your day streak, and whether you cleared today's daily level;
  • your sound and vibration settings;
  • how many levels you cleared since the last full-screen ad;
  • on Android, your answer to the personalized-ads question and whether you bought Remove ads. On iPhone, iOS itself keeps your answer to its tracking question.

Google's consent form, where it is shown, also keeps your answers on your phone.

None of this is sent to us. We run no server, and the game contains no analytics or crash-reporting tools of its own.

If Android backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include this file in your Google account's backup. Google's terms for that backup apply. On iPhone, the file can be part of your iCloud or computer backup, under Apple's terms.

Advertising with Google AdMob

The game shows ads through Google AdMob, a service provided by Google: banners on the menu screens, a full-screen ad between some levels, and an optional video you can watch to skip a level.

When the game requests an ad, Google's software in the game collects and processes:

  • your device's advertising identifier: on Android, the advertising ID; on iPhone, Apple's Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA), but only if you allow tracking. Google may also use identifiers that are limited to this app or to us as its developer;
  • your IP address, and the approximate location Google derives from it;
  • device and app information, such as phone model, Android or iOS version, language and app version;
  • how you interact with ads, such as views and taps, and which ads you have seen;
  • information about how Google's ad software itself performs in the game, such as load times and crash reports.

Google uses this data to show ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure how ads perform, detect fraud and abuse, and improve its ad software. With your consent, Google also uses it to show ads based on your interests, which on iPhone counts as "tracking" under Apple's rules. Without consent you still see ads, but they are not personalized.

We do not receive this data ourselves. AdMob reports only totals to us, such as how many ads were shown.

Google explains its use of this data in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices about ads

  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the game shows Google's consent form the first time you open it. No ads are requested until you answer.
  • Everywhere else, including Türkiye, you are asked whether ads may be personalized. On Android the game asks; both answers look the same. On iPhone, iOS asks whether the game may track you ("Allow" or "Ask App Not to Track"). The game plays the same either way. No ads are requested until you answer; if you do not allow it, ads are not personalized.
  • On iPhone, in every country, iOS asks once whether the game may track you. Apple's advertising identifier is shared with Google only if you choose "Allow".
  • Change your answer, or opt out of the sale or sharing of your data in US states with privacy laws, at any time under Ad choices on the game's title screen. On iPhone, Ad choices opens Google's form where it applies, and otherwise the game's page in the Settings app, where you can turn Allow Tracking off.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off ad personalization, in Android's settings (usually Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads).
  • On iPhone, you can turn off tracking for this game, or stop all apps from asking, in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Remove ads (Android only) turns off banners and the full-screen ads between levels, and skipping a level no longer asks you to watch a video.

The Remove ads purchase (Android)

The iOS version has no purchases. On Android, you can buy Remove ads once, through Google Play. Google Play processes the payment and your payment details under the Google Play Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

The game receives only Google Play's confirmation that your Google account owns the purchase. It checks this each time it starts, so the purchase carries over to a new phone. In Google Play Console we can see order records, such as the order number, date, price and country, which we use only for accounting, tax and refunds.

International transfers

Google processes ad and purchase data on servers in many countries, including the United States.

  • From the EEA and Switzerland, Google relies on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses and the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.
  • From Türkiye, transfers abroad fall under KVKK Article 9 and rely on the safeguards Google provides in its terms.

How long data is kept

  • On your phone: until you delete the game, or on Android clear its data in settings.
  • At Google: for the periods in How Google retains data it collects.
  • Order records in Play Console: as long as tax and accounting law requires.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may ask to access, correct, delete or restrict your data, object to its processing, receive a copy, and withdraw consent at any time.

Under KVKK Article 11 you may ask whether your data is processed and for information about it, learn its purpose and the recipients in Türkiye and abroad, ask for correction or deletion, object to a result reached by automated analysis alone, and claim compensation for damage from unlawful processing.

We keep no personal data about you on our own systems, so most requests concern Google. You can manage them directly in My Ad Center and Google's privacy tools. For anything else, e-mail info@canvider.com; we answer within 30 days.

You can also complain to a data protection authority: in Türkiye the Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK), in the EEA the authority in your country.

US state privacy rights

If you live in a US state with a consumer privacy law, such as California, Virginia, Colorado or Connecticut, you can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your personal information for targeted advertising. Tap Ad choices on the game's title screen and choose to opt out; Google then restricts how it uses your data for ads. On iPhone, choosing "Ask App Not to Track", or turning tracking off later in Settings, also stops Apple's advertising identifier from being shared.

You may also have the right to know what personal information is collected about you and to ask for it to be deleted, as described under Your rights. Using these rights will not change how the game works for you.

Children

The game is meant for players aged 13 and over and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly process children's personal data. If you are a parent and believe your child has used the game, contact us and we will help.
